About This Cup

The Whisper Tea Cup is wheel-thrown by hand at Bacera's Hanoi studio. Each piece begins as raw stoneware clay, shaped slowly on the wheel, then trimmed, bisque-fired, and glazed by hand before its final firing at 1220°C.

The reactive glaze – our signature Brown Earth formula – responds to heat in unpredictable ways during firing, creating the gentle gradients and surface variations that make each cup distinct. No two cups are identical.

Designed For Slow Moments

With a capacity of 70ml, this cup is sized for small-format teas: single-origin whites, aged oolongs, and delicate greens. The thick walls retain heat beautifully, while the slightly flared rim invites full sensory appreciation of the steam and aroma.

  • Kiln-fired at 1220°C for durability
  • Food-safe, lead-free glaze
  • Natural surface variation – imperfections are intentional
  • Made in Hanoi, Vietnam

Crafted with intention, fired with patience
